Facing similar challenges?

All your capital is in one world

Fast growth at home in the Caucasus or Central Asia, but everything sits in one currency, one legal system and one market. You want a stable asset in the US.

Returns at home are thin

Mature US markets are safe but yield little. Economies across the Caucasus & Central Asia are among the faster growing in the world, and you want a share of that growth.

Every trade needs working capital

Each new cargo ties up cash long before your buyer pays. Bank limits rarely grow as fast as your order book.

No one you trust on the other side

Different law, language, banks and habits. You need partners who actually do business there, not a directory of contacts.

Why us

We know both worlds because we do business in both.

Our partners were born in Georgia, studied in England, and live in the United States and France. We run our own businesses in the region and in the West, so we know how deals actually get done on each side, not just the culture.

  • Roots in the region. Native languages, family and business ties across the Caucasus and Central Asia.
  • Western training and base. Educated in the UK, working from the US and Europe.
  • Skin in the game. Our own operating businesses and real estate in both worlds.
  • A network on every side. Investors, banks, traders and advisers in the US, Europe, the Caucasus and Asia.
  • Local partner on the ground. K+A Consulting in Tbilisi handles execution in the region.
