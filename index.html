How a deal moves

From first message to closing

  1. Enquiry

    You send the outline through a short form. We check fit against what our partners actually do.

  2. NDA

    If there is a fit, we sign mutual confidentiality before any names or documents change hands.

  3. Mandate

    A short engagement letter sets out scope, fees and who we introduce you to.

  4. Introduction

    We present your file to the counterparty and manage questions and diligence.

  5. Closing

    Lawyers, banks and licensed partners complete the transaction. We stay involved until it closes.

How we work

Plain rules
