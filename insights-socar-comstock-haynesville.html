SOCAR buys into US shale gas for USD 1.65 billion

Azerbaijan’s state energy company has agreed to take non-operated stakes in Comstock Resources’ Haynesville gas assets in North Louisiana and East Texas, with Comstock remaining operator.

What was signed

SOCAR, the State Oil Company of Azerbaijan, signed a final purchase and sale agreement with Comstock Resources (NYSE: CRK) on 4 November 2026 and announced it the next day. The aggregate cash price is USD 1.65 billion, subject to customary purchase price adjustments, with an effective date of 1 July 2026.

SOCAR will acquire 20% of Comstock’s interest in its Legacy Haynesville upstream assets, 15% of its interest in Western Haynesville, and 15% of Comstock’s 73% holding in Pinnacle Gas Services LLC. The interests are non-operated: Comstock stays the operator.

How the deal is built

SOCAR’s share in Western Haynesville steps down to 7.5% after five years, once SOCAR has earned a 15% return on that part of its investment.

Comstock is a natural gas producer focused on the Haynesville shale in North Louisiana and East Texas. SOCAR takes the Pinnacle share without operational management rights.

What comes next

SOCAR says the required government approvals and other legal procedures are under way. No closing date has been published.

What it means for business

  • Capital is now flowing from the region into the US, not only the other way: a Caucasus state company is becoming a direct owner of American energy assets.
  • A large, long-term Azerbaijani position in US gas builds working relationships between banks, advisers and energy companies on both sides.
  • Cross-border investors of this size need legal, financial and real asset support in the US, which widens the field for US–Caucasus advisory work.

Keep in mind: The deal still needs government approvals and has not closed; the price can move with customary adjustments.
