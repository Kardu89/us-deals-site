Common questions

Do you act as a real estate broker in the US?

No. US brokerage requires a state licence. We introduce the parties and manage the process, and licensed brokerage partners handle the regulated part of the transaction.

Can foreigners buy property in Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan?

Rules differ by country and by type of property, especially for land. We check the rules for each deal with local lawyers before anything is signed.
