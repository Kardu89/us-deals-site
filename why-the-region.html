Six reasons investors look here

Growth

The region is forecast to grow at more than 5% in 2026, several times the pace of most developed economies.

The Middle Corridor

The Trans-Caspian route links China and Central Asia with Europe through Kazakhstan, the Caspian, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Turkey, and its volumes are rising fast.

Commodities

Oil and gas in Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan, metals and uranium in Kazakhstan, gold and natural gas in Uzbekistan, grain across the region: real flows that need trade finance.

Familiar legal anchors

Kazakhstan’s Astana International Financial Centre has a court based on English common law and its own arbitration centre.

Real estate demand

Growing capitals, tourism and new residents are driving demand for housing, hotels and offices in Tbilisi, Batumi, Yerevan, Baku, Almaty and Tashkent.

Diversification

For US portfolios, exposure to markets that move differently from the US. For regional investors, a stable US asset alongside home-market growth.

Country by country

Georgia

  • Black Sea ports of Poti and Batumi on the Middle Corridor
  • Corporate profit tax applies only when profits are distributed
  • Fast company registration and an open real estate market

Armenia

  • Fast-growing tech and services sector
  • Gateway between the Caucasus, Iran and the Middle East
  • Active residential market in Yerevan

Azerbaijan

  • Oil and gas exporter on the Caspian
  • Baku port at Alat is a Middle Corridor hub
  • Large public and private development projects in Baku

Kazakhstan

  • Largest economy in Central Asia
  • Oil, metals, uranium and grain exporter
  • AIFC: common law court and arbitration in Astana

Uzbekistan

  • Most populous country in Central Asia
  • Economic opening and reforms since 2017
  • Gold, natural gas, cotton and fast-growing Tashkent
