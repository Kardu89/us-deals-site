Is your trade a fit?

What our partner banks typically look for.

Exchange-traded commodity

Liquid goods with a public market price: oil products, coal, metals, grain, fertilisers. Niche goods without a quoted price usually do not qualify.

Goods as collateral

The bank lends against the cargo and takes control of it. Real estate is generally not accepted as security.

Short tenor

Financing for one trade cycle, typically one to three months, covering a share of the goods’ value.

Known counterparties

A track record with buyers and sellers, and documents that show the flow: contracts, invoices, inspection reports.

Logistics planned

Freight is usually covered by the trader or by extra collateral, so expensive routes need planning up front.

Full compliance

Account opening and KYC with the bank. Sanctioned goods, parties and routes are excluded without exception.

Common questions

Are you a lender?

No. We are an introducer. Credit decisions, pricing and terms belong to the partner bank.

Which banks do you work with?

Several partner banks. We match each trade to the banks whose criteria it fits and share names after an NDA.

Do you finance invoices (factoring)?

Not currently. Our partner banks prefer trades secured by goods they can take control of and sell.

Who can apply?

Traders based in the US and in Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, subject to the bank’s compliance review.

Trading a liquid commodity and need working capital?

Tell us what you trade, in what volumes, and how much financing you need.
