Trade finance

Short-term financing for commodity traders

We introduce traders to partner banks that finance trades secured by the goods themselves. We prepare the file so the bank can give an answer quickly.

What partner banks look for

Is your trade a fit?

Exchange-traded commodity
Liquid goods with a public market price, such as oil products, coal, metals, grain or fertilisers. Niche goods without a quoted price usually do not qualify.
Short tenor
Financing for a single trade cycle, typically one to three months.
Goods as collateral
The bank lends against the cargo and takes control of it. Real estate is generally not accepted as security.
Part of the trade value
The bank finances a share of the goods’ value. Freight and logistics are usually covered by the trader or by extra collateral.
Known counterparties
A track record with buyers and sellers, and documents that show the flow: contracts, invoices, inspection reports.
Full compliance
Account opening and KYC with the bank. Sanctioned goods, parties and routes are excluded.

How it works

From first call to drawdown

  1. Screening

    A short form: what you buy and sell, volumes, counterparties and the amount you need.

  2. File

    We help you prepare a trading profile: flows, history and contract structure.

  3. Bank review

    We present the file to the partner banks that fit and manage their questions.

  4. Account and limit

    The bank opens an account and sets a limit for your counterparties.

  5. Trade

    Each trade is financed against the cargo and repaid when your buyer pays.

Questions

Common questions

Are you a lender?

No. We are an introducer. Credit decisions, pricing and terms belong to the partner bank.

Which banks do you work with?

We work with several partner banks and match each trade to the ones whose criteria it fits. Names are shared after an NDA.

Do you finance invoices (factoring)?

Not currently. Our partner banks prefer trades secured by goods they can take control of and sell.

Can US traders apply?

Yes. US traders and traders from Europe, the Caucasus and Central Asia are all welcome, subject to the bank’s compliance review.

Trading a liquid commodity and need working capital?

Tell us what you trade, in what volumes, and how much financing you need.
