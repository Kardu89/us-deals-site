Uzbekistan and the US sign 54 agreements worth USD 12 billion

The package covers aviation, rail, mining, finance and digital infrastructure, with GE Aerospace, Citigroup, BlackRock and Trafigura among the US-side names.

What was signed

Uzbekistan and the United States have signed 54 agreements on trade, investment and technology cooperation, under which USD 12 billion is to be mobilised. Uzbekistan’s Ministry of Investment, Industry and Trade announced the package on 20 October 2026.

The agreements reach across industrial modernisation, transport and energy infrastructure, critical minerals, finance, digital technology, agriculture, healthcare and education.

Who is involved

GE Aerospace will supply engines for new Uzbekistan Airways aircraft, and Wabtec plans to localise production of railway equipment and components in the country.

On the finance side, Franklin Templeton, Oppenheimer & Co., Citigroup and BlackRock will help Uzbek companies reach international capital markets, bring in institutional investors and finance infrastructure projects.

In mining, ReElement Technologies, Traxys and Trafigura signed on exploration and development of deposits, technology and mineral supplies. Amazon Leo, GE HealthCare and Valmont Industries cover satellite communications, medical technology and farm efficiency.

What comes next

The ministry says putting the agreements into practice is now the priority. Values for individual deals were not published.

What it means for business

  • US asset managers and banks are building direct links with Uzbek companies, widening the investor base for the region’s most populous market.
  • Mining, rail and aviation contracts create demand for trade finance, logistics and supplier services on both sides.
  • For US investors, the package signals strong policy support for doing business in Uzbekistan.

Keep in mind: Agreements of this kind include memoranda and framework deals. The headline amount is a target to be mobilised, not money already invested.
